# Start

Open this page every time you sit down to work. Then pick one other PDF.

1. Write what “done” means in one sentence.
2. Fill **checklist.pdf**. Blank lines mean you are not ready.
3. Pick one track:
   - New project → **new.pdf**
   - Existing project → **inherited.pdf**
   - Questions only, no code → **research.pdf**
4. Stay on that track until the work is finished or you switch on purpose.
5. Give the AI the `context/` folder. You keep the PDFs.
6. You review the result. You decide commit and push.

Do not open the AI until steps 1–3 are done.
