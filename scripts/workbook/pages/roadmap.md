# Roadmap

Do these in order. Each step assumes the one above it is already true.

0. Git on real work. Ask when a fact is missing. You merge.
1. Spec before code: outcome, out of scope, a command that proves it.
2. Short standing notes in the repo (how to install, run, test). Keep them true.
3. When you repeat a motion twice, save it as a skill or a checklist item. Do not paste it into every prompt.
4. The AI must run your checks before it says done.
5. Large diffs get a second reader. You still merge.
6. Parallel agents only when they do not share files, and only with a spec.

Do not skip to step 6 from a blank folder.
